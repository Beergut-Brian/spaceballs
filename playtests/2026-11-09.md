# Playtest 2026-11-09 — Spaceballs v0.0.1-dev.6

Source: typed (Telegram), Kaden. Transcribed by Gary.

## Who played
Kaden + his son, on Kaden's Omarchy desktop through the Open Jar launcher. First playtest.

## What he did first
Launched from the launcher's dev jar. Player 2 joined late: "by the time I got player two in, I was already on my second round."

## Where he got stuck or confused
- "No main menu. Main menu should be on every game that we make. I couldn't find a way to quit and go back to the launcher without force closing the app."
- The upgrades' effect is invisible: "you would choose an upgrade, the ball would go higher. But you couldn't really see like why it was making the ball go higher. Like some shoes might make you run faster, or have some impact on the gameplay."

## What made him laugh
Not reported. Overall: "it was pretty fun!"

## What he repeated on purpose
Not reported.

## What he asked for
1. A main menu in **every** game, with a way to quit back to the launcher.
2. A start screen where P1 and P2 both ready up before the game starts.
3. No forced walk to the ball: "He's good enough on a controller that he can find his way to the ball. I'd love to just be able to run around and then get to the ball when you're ready."
4. Upgrades that visibly change the gameplay (shoes make you run faster, etc.), not just a bigger number.
5. Much better upgrade art, both the card icons and the gear on the character: "The design of the upgrades is really poor though... I think we should spend a lot of time polishing that." Suggested Fable 5.2 for it.
6. No duplicate gear piling up: "You could also get the same upgrade multiple times, and it would just add them to your character. It looked really odd... Boots just kind of got stuck there and wasn't added to the feet." Gear should actually be worn ("a cape, shoes, kneepads, whatever it is"), as a character-library feature other games can use.

World: "I'm liking the design of the world, I think it can definitely be polished, but it's a good first start."

## Kaden's one thing to change
The upgrades: how they look on the cards and on the character, and that they change how you play.

## Verdict
- [x] At least as fun as current `main` (there is no playable `main` yet)
- [ ] Nothing on the kid-design checklist regressed: n/a (first playtest)
- [ ] Promote `dev` → `main`: **no**, not until menus, ready-up and free movement are in

## Gary: tasks created from this report
1. **Template (every game):** main menu (play, quit to launcher), pause menu with "back to launcher", and a ready-up lobby (each player presses A, both ready → start; solo mode readies both). Text-free. Then sync into Spaceballs. Lane `openjar-template feature/systems-menus-lobby`.
2. **Character library:** a real equipment system for Jarlings: named slots (head, face, back/cape, hands, knees, feet, waist) fitted to the body and following the rig; one item per slot (a new item replaces the old); a level-up look for a repeat. Lane `openjar-characters feature/equipment-slots`.
3. **Spaceballs gameplay:** free movement (run anywhere; the kick fires when you run into your ball); every upgrade gets a visible gameplay effect (run speed, run-up length, jump, kick style, ball behaviour) on top of its height; no duplicate cards for gear you already wear. Lane `spaceballs feature/gameplay-free-roam-upgrades`.
4. **Upgrade art redo (Fable 5.2):** card icons and wearable gear rebuilt on the equipment system. Starts after 2 lands.
5. Later: world polish pass.
