# Playtest 2026-11-09 (#2) — Spaceballs v0.0.1-dev.9

Source: typed (Telegram), Kaden. Transcribed by Gary.

## Who played
Kaden (and his son) on the Omarchy desktop through the launcher.

## Overall
"A lot of the improvements have made noticeable positive changes to the game!"

## Where it fell short
- The run-up is too short for the new upgrade effects: "some of the fun aspects that are now involved with the changes to how the cards impact the gameplay, are nerfed by the fact that you're so close to the ball. Especially things that make you bounce, it's fun and cool, but you're so close to the ball, it hardly has a chance to register."

## What he asked for
1. "Move that ball way further away so that you have much more lead up time to get there and actually kind of play round with the animation and the gameplay effects."
2. Curses happen to you, not picked: "I'd like the curse cards to show up in the actual game. In an average game, let's have two curse cards come up. It shouldn't be a card that you pick, there should just be a chance that the card you picked is actually a curse card."
3. Banana peel: "should be moved a little bit further out from the ball so that it's obvious that you trip and then trip into the ball and then it doesn't really go anywhere."
4. Sky: "more objects in the sky, and if the ball could interact with them a little bit more. Like popping a balloon, hitting an airplane, things like that."
5. Bystander: "let the other character run around with their upgrades as well when it's the other person's turn, they just can't kick any of the balls."
6. Crowd: "the spectators on the side, when they see a good kick, to cheer and celebrate."

## Verdict
- [x] Better than the last build
- [ ] Promote `dev` → `main`: not yet (these asks first)

## Gary: tasks created from this report
1. Gameplay lane `feature/gameplay-playtest-2`: longer field and run-up (camera follows the run), hidden curses (about 2 per run, revealed after a normal pick), banana trip from further out, bystander runs with full upgrades and can't kick, crowd cheers scaled to kick quality.
2. Sky lane `feature/sky-objects`: more sky objects per tier and ball interactions (pop a balloon, bonk a plane, scatter birds, bounce off a cloud, a satellite ping), every one with a sound.
