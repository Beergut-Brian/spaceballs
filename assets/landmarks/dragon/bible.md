# The Dragon

**Kind:** universal. **Playable:** false. **Default for:** none. **Body:** giant. **First appears:** The Hill (the landmark).

## Who they are
The Dragon is enormous, slow, yawning, and wants to be friends. She is sleepy and huggable from the first frame, and never scary: no visible teeth, no sharp claws, no fire. She snores smoke rings, and sometimes they are heart-shaped. She likes being built on.

## In three sentences
The Dragon has been asleep at the Bottom so long that her snore has been shaking the hill all game. She is not sorry on purpose; she is just asleep, and then very sorry. Her wings are far too small and she flaps them anyway.

## How they sound
Barks only, no words: low rumbles, big yawns, a purr, and the snore, which is low and shakes the screen.

## How they move and react
Everything takes two seconds. She yawns before every action. She flaps the tiny wings and nothing happens. She nods off mid-gesture and wakes up looking pleased.

## Signature move
The yawn, then the slow, gentle wave of one stubby arm, then the nap.

## What they love
Naps. Being built on. Warm rocks. The players.

## What they hate
Nothing. Alarm clocks would be a candidate.

## Running gags
She falls asleep mid-gesture. Smoke rings drift over the town. The gnomes hang lanterns on her. The elevator carries her up in pieces, tail first.

## Relationships
Jeff hugs her and she purrs. Chuck is wary at first, then falls asleep leaning on her. Among the residents, the Foreman hangs lanterns on her, the Caveman polishes her horn nubs, and the Mole keeps apologising to her toes.

## Redemption arc
**Reusable part.** Any game can use this beat: a nuisance becomes a friend in three steps (Encounter, Arrival, Fix-up), told in picture bubbles with no words. Always optional, funny-sad and never sad: no crying, no begging, and they stay funny while waiting. Afterwards they give something back.

### The Hill's version
Encounter: she is asleep at the Bottom, curled round the last treasure. Arrival: she wakes with a yawn, rides up in pieces, and waits on the peak. The bubble shows the shaken hill with a sheepish face, then the Ruined Keep with a hopeful face. Fix-up: the keep becomes the Dragon Perch. She warms the Ice layer and the town in winter, and she is the landmark.
